# Escritorio para comparación de información

9 octubre 2026. Generación con herramienta integrada de imágenes (no CLI), una propuesta seleccionada. Fotografía conceptual; no representa un producto o cliente real. Marca ficticia solo en HTML. Sin referencias privadas ni datos de terceros.

## Prompt final

Use case: product-mockup. Create a natural editorial photograph for two illustrative website mockups of the same fictional Mexican carpentry workshop. Landscape 3:2. One beautifully crafted light oak compact writing desk, simple slim rectangular top, elegantly rounded corners, one discreet drawer, slender solid wood legs. Desk is against an off-white plaster wall in a small welcoming apartment, chair tucked partly beneath, daylight from left. A notebook and simple ceramic cup on desk, one small plant at side, no people. Full desk visible including legs and enough wall/floor around it, eye-level three-quarter view. Warm sophisticated but believable, real wood grain, restrained palette warm oak, linen, muted forest green. Not a luxury palace, not cluttered. No text, no logos, no labels, no inset graphics, no watermark. This is a conceptual product photo, not evidence of a real client's work. It will be reused unchanged in BOTH panels to isolate information quality, so show one desk only, no split composition.
